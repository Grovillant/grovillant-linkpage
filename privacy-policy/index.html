Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Grovillant ("we", "us", or "our") uses an internal application connected to the Amazon Ads API solely to manage, analyze, and optimize our own Amazon advertising accounts. This application is not offered as a third-party advertising service.

1. Information We Access

Through the Amazon Ads API, our internal application may access advertising-related information associated with our own Amazon advertising accounts, including:

  • Advertising account and profile identifiers;
  • Campaign, ad group, keyword, targeting, bid, and budget settings;
  • Advertising performance metrics and reporting data; and
  • Authorization credentials or tokens required to maintain the API connection.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information only for legitimate internal business purposes, including:

  • Advertising reporting and analytics;
  • Campaign creation and management;
  • Bid, budget, keyword, and targeting optimization; and
  • Internal workflow automation related to our own Amazon advertising operations.

3. Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell Amazon Ads API data or personal information. We do not use Amazon Ads API data for unrelated purposes. Information may be disclosed only when necessary to operate our internal systems, comply with applicable law, or comply with Amazon policies and requirements.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. Access to API credentials and advertising data is limited to authorized personnel and systems with a business need.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for our internal advertising operations, reporting, compliance, security, and legitimate business purposes, subject to applicable laws and Amazon requirements.

6. Third-Party Services

Our internal application interacts with Amazon services, including the Amazon Ads API and Login with Amazon. Use of Amazon services is also subject to Amazon's applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
